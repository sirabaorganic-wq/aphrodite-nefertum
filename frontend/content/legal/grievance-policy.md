## 6. Information Required

To facilitate investigation, customers should provide:

- Full Name
- Order Number
- Contact Information
- Date of Purchase
- Product Name
- Description of Issue
- Supporting Photographs
- Supporting Documents
- Unboxing Video (if applicable)
Failure to provide adequate information may delay resolution.
---

## 7. Complaint Acknowledgment

Upon receipt of a complaint:

- Acknowledgment may be provided within 48 business hours.
- A reference number may be assigned.
- Additional information may be requested if necessary.
Acknowledgment does not imply acceptance of the complaint.
---

## 8. Investigation Process

The Company may:

- Review order records
- Examine shipping records
- Evaluate photographs and videos
- Consult logistics providers
- Consult manufacturers or suppliers
- Request additional evidence
The Company reserves the right to determine appropriate investigation procedures.
---

## 9. Resolution Timeline

The Company aims to resolve complaints within:

Standard Complaints

7–15 Business Days

Complex Complaints

15–30 Business Days

Actual timelines may vary depending on complexity.
---

## 10. Possible Resolution Outcomes

Depending on the circumstances, the Company may:

- Replace a product
- Issue a refund
- Provide store credit
- Offer technical assistance
- Reject unsupported claims
- Close complaints lacking sufficient evidence
All decisions shall be made in accordance with applicable policies.
---

## 11. Product Damage Claims

For claims involving damaged products:

Customers must provide:

- Complete unboxing video
- Photographs of packaging
- Photographs of damaged product
Claims submitted without reasonable evidence may be rejected.
---

## 12. Fraudulent Complaints

The Company reserves the right to reject complaints that:

- Contain false information
- Include manipulated evidence
- Involve abuse of company policies
- Are submitted in bad faith
Fraudulent activity may result in account restrictions or legal action.
---

## 13. Customer Conduct

Customers are expected to communicate respectfully.

The Company reserves the right to terminate communications involving:

- Abusive language
- Threats
- Harassment
- Defamation
- Discriminatory conduct
This does not limit legitimate customer rights under applicable law.
