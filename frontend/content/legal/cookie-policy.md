## 2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your computer, smartphone, tablet, or other device when you visit a website.

Cookies help websites:

- Function properly
- Improve user experience
- Remember preferences
- Analyze visitor behavior
- Support advertising and marketing activities
Cookies do not typically contain information that directly identifies an individual, but may be linked with personal information collected by the Website.
